# MMO Stage 6: mining, smithing and salvaging

Date: 2026-10-20 · Agent: Claude Code · Commit: Mining, smithing and salvaging behind GAME_FEATURES=mmoCrafting (MMO Stage 6)

## Why
The last MMO stage in the roadmap (docs/game-design/mmo/07): soft skills that feed the Stage 3 materials and refinement.

## Decisions and evidence
- Three skills only: mining, smithing and salvaging. Fishing, cooking and herbalism from doc 07 were left out: there are no assets for them, and nothing in the item economy uses them.
- The XP curve uses the RuneScape-style formula: level 2 at 83 XP, about 13 M XP at 99, capped at 100. Skill XP is stored in the `characters.skills` jsonb column (migration 009) and sanitised on load.
- Veins are placed deterministically by `oreNodes(act, arena)`:
  - one vein 1.5 m beside a spread of the act's hostile placements, at most MAX_NODES;
  - ore kinds per place prefix: `ca` caves, `cm` mines, `gl` glacier, `co`, `du`;
  - towns and other places get no veins.
  - Every zone of the same act agrees on the veins, and no data file was added.
- A vein holds 3–5 charges. Once worked out it comes back after 120–300 s, divided by √(players/10).
- One harvest every 2.5 s, with ±0.3 s jitter so a fixed-rate macro drifts.
- The server checks reach: 2.5 m, plus 0.5 m of slack in the plane, and up to 2.5 m vertically.
- Smithing and salvaging need an open merchant (`p.shop`): the merchant's anvil, so no new world object is needed.
- Smithing a recipe:
  - takes its inputs and gold, after checking on a copy that the result will fit in the bag;
  - with `mmoItems` on, the forged gear rolls rarity like a find, with the first roll lowered by `smithRarityBonus(level)`.
- Salvaging refuses anything `untradable()` refuses (worn, key, quest or bound items). The yield comes from the item's rarity, via the SALVAGE table.

## Deviations from the original
- Everything here is non-original and off unless `GAME_FEATURES` includes `mmoCrafting`.
- The veins are primitive meshes, not original art.
- The anvil is the ⚒ button on a merchant's window, shown only when the feature is on.
- Tab mines the nearest vein.

## Not done / not decoded
- The recipe numbers (inputs, gold, XP and levels) are first-pass values; doc 10's economy scenarios were not re-run against them.
- Not played live in the browser; covered by unit tests only.

## Gotchas
- `oreNodes` keys on the act prefix (`act.slice(0,2)`). A new act family needs an entry in ACT_ORES.
- Vein state lives in the zone and is not saved: a zone restart refills every vein.
- Dungeon instances (`level:ca1d@crypt-N`) resolve to the base act, so they get veins too.
- `craftReadyTick` is per player, not per vein.

## How to verify
- Run `npx vitest run unit/crafting.test.ts` in `tests/`.
- Live check:
  - set `GAME_FEATURES=mmoCrafting` and go to any `cm` act (the mines);
  - the veins show grey rocks with coloured crystals;
  - Tab mines a vein;
  - open a merchant and use ⚒ to forge or salvage.
