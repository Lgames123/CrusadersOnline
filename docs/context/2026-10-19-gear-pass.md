# Gear pass: equip bonuses, shield masks, soul drinking, item looks

Date: 2026-10-19 · Agent: Claude Code · Commit: Gear as the original: equip bonuses on every worn item, shield use masks, soul-drinking items, weapon and armour looks

## Why
Complete pass over items: many items.ctx properties were read only for rings (Shield of Mighty Deeds, Enoch's Plate, Fist of Necros did nothing), shields' protection and uses were ignored, Boomer Axe / Dasher Hand Bow were missing (left out of shops and drops), and texture variants / armour skins were wrong.

## Decisions and evidence
- Decoded in executable-analysis §23 (use factory 0x00402170, mask test 0x00401F20, DRINK_SOUL 0x004014C0, REFLECT 0x00401810, weapon use on landed blows 0x0044D59A).
- `Equip BOOST_*` now applies to weapons, shields and armour (`withEquipBoost`), shields keep `SetProtection` (Ice / Fire Shield, Shield of Sanctuary…).
- Shields carry `blocks` (USE_MASK). Melee: a raised shield stops a blow only if its mask has MELEE, or GIANTS for types 0x87–0x8A / scale ≥ 1.5 — so **plain shields no longer stop Ice Titans, elementals, or the 1.5×+ skeletons/ogres** (as the original). Fire Shield stops explosions; Reflective Shield stops bolts (no damage) and strikes the caster back (chain ends).
- `soulDrink`: Blade of Hunger 1 (+giants), Rod of Necros 5, Vampiric Shield 3 on stopped blows; creatures holding them drink too. Shared `drain`/`heal` of the spell host.
- Calls inherit by name in `catalogFromItemRules` (items.json drops a parent's calls when the child has any): `armorSkin` from `SetArmorSkin`. Client reads the same chain: Plate of Sanctuary / Elemental Protection / Enoch's wear the heavy plate body, Dragon Scale its own texture.
- items.ctx `Name` is the displayed name (Soul Sucker → "Blade of Hunger", Invisibility Potion → "Lesser Healing Potion"); ids unchanged.
- THROWABLE / MISSILE weapons are swung in Drake's hand (no ASM_THROW_ITEM clip for him, no Arrow anywhere). Every placed, carried and shop item now resolves in the catalog (checked over all placements).
- Held items take their texture variant (`file#texture` in DrakeOutfit; MODEL_TEXTURES 71, 76, 77, 99, 244 from models.ctx). Tooltips list effects and protections above 1 (Dragon Scale's LIGHT/DARK 1.2).

## Deviations from the original
- Reflect returning the bolt to its caster is an interpretation of REFLECT's `+0x54/+0x60` call; melee REFLECT does nothing extra.

## Not done / not decoded
- Boomer Axe: each landed blow should also throw a MAGIC_AXE_PROJ projectile (type 3, 60 ft/s); its flight/damage/return not decoded, not implemented.
- Normal shields vs creature projectiles (PROJECTILE bit, the creatures' fire ball missiles) not wired; only explosions and bolts ask the shield.
- Creatures' own shield protection / held weapon equip boosts are not applied (their placement resist is used).
- Never-placed items (Ceremonial Sword, Family Tree Sword, Hammer of Bolga, Fain's Axe, Magic/Asok's Ring, Royal Longsword, Fire Shield, Rod of Necros, Extra *) stay catalogue-only (admin grant); side quests that give some are unshipped.

## Gotchas
- DEV_GUEST_ITEMS gives items no grid position: swaps can report "Inventory full." in a fragmented bag (pre-existing).

## How to verify
- `pnpm test:unit` (gear.test.ts).
- In game: block an Ice Titan with a Wooden Shield (hits land); wear Rod of Necros (soul drinker sparks, HP rises per hit); wear Plate of Sanctuary (heavy plate body) / Shadow Blade (dark blade).
